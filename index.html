Data & Computational Social Scientist

I'm a published dual-title Ph.D. candidate in Political Science and Social Data Analytics. My work applies prediction, machine learning and natural language processing to social science outcomes. My dissertation examines the diffusion of state firearm regulations, evaluates how well policy diffusion models predict, and uncovers length-dependent biases in text classification.
