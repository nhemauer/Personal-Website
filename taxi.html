Project demo

NYC Taxi Agent

Ask about NYC yellow taxi trips from 2011 to today. A Gemini agent writes and runs BigQuery SQL to answer, estimates fares with a random forest, and forecasts future trips with histogram gradient boosting. See the code.

The data

Every yellow taxi trip since 2011

Public trip records from the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission, aggregated in BigQuery, and updated automatically each week by a scheduled Cloud Run job, with CI/CD through Cloud Build. Set up with GitHub Actions to deploy the website.

  • Hourly totals of trips and fares, counted from every trip, for exact figures over any period.
  • A 1-in-1,000 sample of individual trips, for details such as distances, tips, and payment types.

Fare estimates

What would a trip cost?

Give a distance, pickup time, trip length, and number of passengers, and a random forest predicts the metered fare (before tips, tolls, and surcharges).

  • Model trained on 1.3 million sampled trips from 2011 to 2026.
  • On held-out trips it was off by $0.54 on average (R² 0.94).

Forecasts

How busy will it be?

Forecasts trips and average fares for future hours, days, weeks, or months, each with a likely range, and the busiest times of day.

  • Histogram Gradient-Boosted models trained on hourly data since 2023 purposely to avoid COVID-era ride changes and NYC fare changes. Inputs are time of day, day of week, season, and holidays.
  • On the last 3 months, held out, trip counts were off by 7.9% per hour, beating a “same week last year” guess (10.6%).
